01 · Profile

About me

I'm a network and system integration engineer, and for the last few years I've had my hands on the networks that keep large industrial sites running. That means designing and securing infrastructure built on Cisco, Huawei, Juniper, Aruba, and Hughes (VSAT/NMS), and running the day-to-day: firewalls like FortiGate and Juniper, monitoring with Splunk, Trellix, WhatsUp Gold, and Nozomi, and core services like Active Directory, DNS, and DHCP.

Unified communications is another area I know well, especially Cisco CUCM and SIP-based telephony. I've also integrated CCTV, VPN, and RF systems into production networks. I approach every deployment the same way: make it redundant, keep it secure, and don't let it fail quietly.
